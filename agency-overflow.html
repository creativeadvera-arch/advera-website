Clear inputs. Clear handover.

1. Brief

Share one high-level, non-confidential goal, the asset count and required formats. We confirm tool compatibility and whether the studio can take the job.

2. Agree

Approve the exact deliverables, revision limit, licensed source materials, allowed AI assistance, confidentiality, fee and target date before any work starts.

3. Review & deliver

Receive agreed export files and an editable Canva link, with checks for wording, sizing, consistency and permitted imagery. No contact with your end client unless agreed.
