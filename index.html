Questions, answered

Confidence before commitment.

A good creative partnership should be easy to understand. Here are the details clients commonly want clarified before getting started.

Ask us something else ↗
What exactly will I receive?

Each package specifies the number of distinct concepts, placement sizes and revision rounds. The Starter, for example, delivers three different static directions adapted to Feed and Stories, with six exports and editable Canva files. We confirm the file formats and handover details in your brief so nothing important is left to assumption.

Can you create videos and Reels too?

We can help shape the creative foundation of a video campaign—hooks, messaging, scripts and storyboards. Our published packages currently focus on static ads, where we have a defined production process. If you need finished video, share your brief and footage requirements; we'll discuss whether a separately scoped production solution is appropriate rather than promise a capability or delivery we haven't verified.

Do you manage Meta ad campaigns?

Our core service is the creative: ideas, copy and ad assets that your media buyer or internal team can test. That keeps budgets and responsibilities clear. If you need campaign setup or ongoing media buying, tell us and we can help define what additional expertise would be required; it isn't silently bundled into a design quote.

Can you work with our existing brand assets?

Absolutely. Working with your approved photos, product details, logos, colours, tone of voice and brand guidelines is our preferred approach. It helps preserve brand consistency and avoid expensive reshoots. We confirm image rights and any required disclosures before production begins.

What if we don't have professional photos?

We can first assess the product imagery you already have and suggest layouts that make sensible use of it. If additional imagery, licensed stock or original photography is required, we'll flag that before quoting and agree what can be created lawfully and within budget. We won't assume permission to use someone else's images.

How quickly can we start and deliver?

We'll confirm a realistic schedule once we've reviewed the brief, available assets and current workload. Straightforward static packages are designed to be efficient, but we'll agree the delivery date with you rather than advertise a blanket turnaround that might compromise quality.

What if we want changes?

One consolidated revision round is included in the listed Starter and Creative Engine packages. Before starting, we'll define what counts as a revision versus a new concept or changed brief. If you need additional options, we'll quote the extra work clearly so you retain control over spend.

Will the ads definitely improve sales or conversions?

Our approach is to create meaningful variations that your team can test, learn from and refine. Commercial results depend on the product, offer, targeting, media spend, landing page and many other factors. We don't make unsupported performance guarantees. If you share campaign findings later, we can use them to inform the next round of creative.

Do you work with agencies or overseas businesses?

Yes. Advera is based in Perth, Australia and can collaborate remotely with brands and agencies in other markets. We offer brief-led white-label creative with an agreed handover process, confidentiality terms, timezone expectations and no direct end-client contact unless requested.

How do payments and ownership work?

We confirm the quote, payment milestones, deliverables, usage rights and editable-file handover in writing before production. We'll also clarify any third-party licensing restrictions, especially for client-supplied or stock assets. That way the commercial arrangement matches how you intend to use the work.

Can we start small before choosing a monthly plan?

Absolutely. Start with the A$99 single-concept pilot (one original static idea adapted to Feed and Story, editable Canva, one consolidated minor revision), or the A$299 Creative Starter (three distinct concepts, six exports, one revision round). Neither requires a monthly commitment. We confirm the brief, approved assets, exact revision scope, delivery date and payment terms before production.
