Privacy and business enquiries.

Updated 9 November 2026. This notice explains the information involved when you visit our website or contact us about creative services.

Information you choose to send

Advera Creative receives the contact details, company information, campaign briefs, brand materials and other details you voluntarily share by email. The website's contact buttons open an email message in your email application; this site does not currently offer an on-site submission form, customer account or checkout.

How we use enquiry information

We use information provided to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, communicate about requested work and, if we agree to a project, prepare and deliver its creative assets and maintain relevant project or accounting records. We do not publish client work, names, testimonials or results without separate permission.

Service providers and technical data

Our website is hosted on Render, which may process routine technical request information needed to serve and protect the site. The website also loads Google Fonts, which may involve requests to Google. Email conversations are processed through our email provider. Where appropriate for an agreed project, clients may receive Canva links or files using relevant third-party services; please avoid sending sensitive information we don't need for your brief.

Your choices

You're welcome to ask what information you've sent us, request a correction, or discuss deletion of enquiry details where appropriate, subject to any legitimate business, legal or accounting retention needs. We may need to verify that a request comes from the relevant contact before acting.

Contact

For business enquiries or privacy questions, email creativeadvera@gmail.com.

This page may be updated as our tools, hosting and services change. Any important change should be reflected here before we rely on a new data-collection workflow.
