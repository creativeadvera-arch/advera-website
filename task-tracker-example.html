What this workbook demonstrates

Consistent status inputs

Drop-down choices for priority and status reduce conflicting terminology and help staff enter updates consistently.

Formula-driven overview

Counts for completed, open and overdue items are calculated from task rows instead of retyped into an executive summary.

Clear warning flags

Due-soon and overdue fields change according to due dates and completion status. The original source remains editable.
