Inside the draft bundle

Practical pieces for everyday quoting.

Built as an editable starting point, not a replacement for accounting, job management, or customer relationship management software.

Excel lead tracker

Example enquiries, follow-up dates and quote stages organised in a reusable workbook. Adapt it to your own workflow.

Example copy

Helpful words without the awkward chase.

Two examples from the preview. Always check details, timing, privacy and consent before sending customer communications.

New enquiry acknowledgment
“Hi [First name], thanks for contacting [Company] about [project]. We have your enquiry. Could you share [one missing detail] so we can give you an accurate next step?”
Illustrative editable message. No automated messaging service is included.
After sending a quote
“Hi [First name], checking whether you had any questions on quote [reference]. The main variable that affects the price is [relevant factor]. I can explain your options.”
Personalise wording and timing for the actual customer relationship.
