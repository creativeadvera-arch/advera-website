Questions we expect.

Is this really free? What's the catch?

For an accepted and confirmed founding-client pilot, the agreed project costs A$0. There is no compulsory review, credit-card request or paid upsell. We may offer a separate quote for additional work only if you ask. This is a limited trial to test our process and build real delivery experience.

Are two places guaranteed to be available?

No. Up to two projects may be accepted, subject to eligibility and capacity. An email application does not reserve a place; we confirm acceptance and the deliverables in writing. We will remove or update the invitation after the program is filled or closed.

Will you publish our project?

Not without separate written permission. You can accept the free work and decline publicity or feedback entirely. We don't publish confidential project data or claim that fictional examples were client work.

What if my job is bigger?

We will explain the limitation and, if appropriate, provide an optional fixed-scope paid quote. You may decline it. We will not begin paid work or issue invoices without mutually agreed scope and payment arrangements.

Have a small task that would make your week easier?

Send a short description with your business name, website or public profile and preferred option. We'll reply only if we can assess the request. Please do not send personal information about third parties or business-confidential source files in the initial email.
