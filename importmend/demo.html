Example B: a risky product update

Our checks flagged an invalid price, incomplete variant identity and missing first-row title in a separate hypothetical update file. Because the audit detected blockers, the result is NOT approved for import.

  • Invalid price: verify numeric source values, rather than fabricate a replacement.
  • Missing variant options: avoid updating SKUs without original option fields.
  • Missing product title: correct the first product row before attempting an update.
