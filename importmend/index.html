Frequently asked questions

Will you change my live Shopify store?

No. This pilot prepares files offline; your team reviews Shopify's actual import preview and makes the upload decision.

What if product details are missing?

We flag the missing information. We never invent prices, barcodes, variants, taxonomies or descriptions.

Can this guarantee a successful import?

No. CSV checks cannot verify store-specific apps, current product data, image hosting or Shopify's live import results.

When do I pay?

First we confirm fit, a secure transfer process, scope, payment arrangements and order terms. This website is an enquiry page, not a checkout.
