A safer process

Clear input. Checked output. No surprise changes.

01 · Define

Tell us the problem, file type and approximate scope. We clarify what can be changed, what must stay untouched and whether AI assistance is permitted.

02 · Protect

Keep an untouched backup. Share approved example data rather than personal, payroll or commercially sensitive records until an appropriate handling process is agreed.

03 · Handover

We provide an editable file, a short change summary and an agreed correction round. Your team reviews and validates it before putting it into daily operations.
