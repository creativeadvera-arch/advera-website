Before you get started

A few fair questions.

Why pay when tender notices are free?

You don't have to. The free board links to official sources. A paid brief covers tailored searching, screening, a source-backed fit assessment and a clear list of unresolved checks for your business.

Will a brief guarantee work or eligibility?

No. You decide whether to bid, and the issuing organisation decides the outcome. We distinguish a public listing from eligibility and flag what needs checking in the tender pack.

Do you write bids or submit tenders?

The pilot is research only. Tender writing, pricing your work, technical advice, registrations, document purchases and submissions are outside the listed scope.

How is research checked?

We use AI-assisted research and review the public source links and dates. Each brief labels source facts, our assessment and unknowns separately. The source-check timestamp is not a promise of continuous monitoring.

What happens after I enquire?

We confirm whether the scope is feasible, the deliverables, availability, final price and payment arrangements before work begins. Sending an enquiry does not place an order.
