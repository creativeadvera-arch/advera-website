The process

Less confusion. Cleaner handover.

STEP 01

Send a short brief

Describe the problem, approximate file or content volume, deadline and approved inputs. For privacy, do not send personal or confidential source files in the first email.

STEP 02

Agree written terms

We review feasibility and confirm a fixed deliverable list, exact fee, date, one-revision boundary, asset rights and safe payment arrangements before any work begins.

STEP 03

Receive reviewed files

We validate the output against the agreed source facts, preserve originals where appropriate and return the editable deliverables with a brief handover.

Important scope and payment note

Enquiries are not orders. Prices start at the amounts shown only where the described scope fits. Tax treatment, supplier details, payment method and delivery schedule are confirmed before acceptance. We will not request banking details, IDs or private client records by ordinary email. AI assistance is used where appropriate and compatible with client permissions.

Frequently asked

Know what's included.

Can I ask for a quote before sharing files?

Yes. Start with approximate row and column counts, product field headers or the number of Canva assets, plus the changes you need. We will discuss secure delivery of source files later if needed.

Do you guarantee sales, SEO ranking or advertising results?

No. The product is checked digital-production work, not guaranteed sales, platform approval or campaign performance.

What if I need a different amount of work?

We'll either propose a clearly priced custom scope or say when the request falls outside our current capacity. Extra sizes, extra revision rounds, complex variants and sensitive data are not silently included.

Do you work with businesses outside Perth?

Yes. We work remotely from Perth with Australian and international clients where the scope, licence, secure intake and payment arrangements are practical.

Are the examples previous paying clients?

No. Published sample creative and file examples are original, self-directed fictional studies designed to show the intended quality and method. We do not claim real customer results that we have not earned.
