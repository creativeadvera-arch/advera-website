Checking your quote…

Minimum quote = (hours × planning rate + direct expenses + target extra profit) ÷ (1 − fee rate). Your estimated profit also subtracts the planning value of your own hours; it is not accounting profit or take-home pay. Taxes and shared overhead aren't included.

How to interpret the numbers

A quote can appear profitable before you assign any value to your own time. This calculator shows whether the proposed price covers a planning allowance for the hours you work, direct job costs, and fees paid as a percentage of the sale. If the estimate falls below your target, consider adjusting scope, hours, price or the assumptions—not just cutting your earnings.

What this calculator does not do

It doesn't calculate tax, insurance, shared overhead, cashflow, superannuation or the true accounting profit of your business. It doesn't save client data, issue invoices, connect bank accounts or guarantee profitable work. Keep sensitive client details out of any online forms. The calculation runs locally on this page.
